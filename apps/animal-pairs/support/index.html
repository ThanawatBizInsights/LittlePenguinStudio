Animal Pairs Support

Animal Pairs is a matching game for iPhone and iPad. If you need help with the game, audio settings, an unlock, or restoring a purchase, we're happy to help.

Contact us

Email support@littlepenguinstudio.com. We usually reply within two business days.

Please include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, and a short description of the issue. Please do not send children's personal information.

Common questions

There is no sound or music.
Open Settings in Animal Pairs and check the separate Music and Sound Effects controls. Also check your device volume, the silent switch or Silent Mode, and which speaker or headphones the device is using.
How do I restore a purchase?
If Full Game Unlock is offered in your version, open the Parents area and choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. [Verify exact navigation and wording in the release build.]
How do I ask for a refund?
Purchases are processed by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
